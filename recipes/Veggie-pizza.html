Veggie Pizza

Description

Veggie pizza is one of the best pizzas in the world. It consists of a thin crust topped with a variety of fresh vegetables.

Ingredients

  • Bread
  • cheese
  • olives
  • tomato
  • onions
  • bell pepper
  • mushrooms

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 475°F (245°C).
  2. Roll out the pizza dough on a floured surface to your desired thickness.
  3. Transfer the rolled-out dough to a pizza stone or baking sheet.
  4. Spread a thin layer of tomato sauce evenly over the dough, leaving a small border around the edges.
  5. Sprinkle a generous amount of shredded mozzarella cheese over the sauce.
  6. Add your choice of sliced vegetables, such as bell peppers, onions, mushrooms, and olives, evenly over the cheese.
  7. Drizzle a little olive oil over the top and season with salt, pepper, and any desired herbs (like oregano or basil).
  8. Bake the pizza in the preheated oven for 12-15 minutes, or until the crust is golden and the cheese is bubbly and slightly browned.
  9. Remove the pizza from the oven and let it cool for a few minutes before slicing and serving.
